# Overview

This HR Dashboard provides insights into the workforce composition, salary distribution, employee growth, and other HR metrics for Awesome Chocolates. It helps HR professionals analyze key workforce statistics effectively.

Features

**Headcount & Salary Insights**: Displays total employee count, average salary, and leave balance statistics.

**Workforce Composition**: Breakdown of employees by job roles.

**Gender Distribution**: Pie chart representing male vs. female staff ratio.

**Age Distribution**: Histogram showing the distribution of employees by age.

**Salary vs. Qualification**: Scatter plot analyzing salary trends based on qualification levels.

**Employee Growth**: Line graph tracking workforce growth over the years.
